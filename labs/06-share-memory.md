# Lab 06: share memory

Read `docs/shared-memory.md` first.

1. With a partner (or alone, with a second repo), create a knowledge repository: `team-knowledge`, with `INDEX.md`, `errors/`, `learnings/`.
2. Pick one entry from your error log that other people would hit too. Remove anything private.
3. Ask your agent to turn it into one file in `team-knowledge/errors/` and open a pull request there.
4. Your partner reviews and merges.
5. In your own `AGENTS.md`, add the path of the knowledge repository and "read INDEX.md first".
6. Test: does your partner's agent now avoid your error?

Record whether your agent could read the sibling folder, as `verified` or `unverified`.
